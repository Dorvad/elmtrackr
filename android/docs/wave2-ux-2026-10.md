# Wave 2 — UX, reporting clarity and everyday usability (October 2026)

Wave 1 (PR #168) made pay, leave and currencies correct. Wave 2 makes them
understandable: what was worked, what is paid, what it is estimated to earn, and
where the data is. Every finding below was verified against source on `Main` at
`045c2dc` before it was changed; earlier audits were used as leads, not as facts.

Branch: `ccr-30ee2d00-yfjh2v` (the session's designated branch, used as the Wave 2
branch). Nothing was merged or released.

---

## 1. The idea that runs through all of it: worked time is not paid time

The engines price `payableNetMinutes`: clock time, less unpaid breaks, then
rounding, then the minimum-shift floor. Before this wave the app showed that
figure under labels that said "Total", "worked" and "hours", and the shift form
called clock time "worked" and clock time less breaks "paid" regardless of the
profile's rules.

`PayrollCalculator.payableTime` now keeps each step as `PaidTime` (elapsed,
recorded break, automatic break, rounding, minimum top-up, payable).
`payableNetMinutes` delegates to it, so the priced number is unchanged — a test
asserts equality across every rule combination, and that
`payable == worked + paidBreak + rounding + minimumTopUp` always holds.

Rules applied everywhere this wave touched:

- **"Worked"** is clock time less every break. It is never rounded or topped up.
- **"Paid time"** appears only when the profile's rules made it differ, with the
  rule named: rounding, the minimum-shift guarantee, paid breaks, or "your work
  profile's time rules" when several apply.
- Money is labelled **"Estimated gross · before tax"**, one figure per currency.

`ShiftClassification.workedMinutes` had been assigned the payable figure despite
its own KDoc; nothing read it then. It now holds worked time.

## 2. What changed, by area

### Dashboard
- Month card: hours are worked hours, labelled "Worked"; a paid-time line only
  when it differs; earnings "Estimated gross · before tax"; "All jobs" when there
  is more than one; an empty month says so instead of drawing a zero bar; without a
  rate the card offers **Set rate**.
- **Clock-out recap** after ending an hourly shift on the dashboard: shift length,
  unpaid break, worked, paid time (if different), estimated pay and its
  components, from `calculateShiftPayInContext` over the same pay-week window as
  the month card. It takes the top of the existing one-nudge slot, opens the shift's
  form for review, and never mentions the cloud.
- **Long-shift notice** at 16 hours: asks whether the clock-out was forgotten and
  opens the form to set the real end time (not "Clock out", which would record the
  wrong end).
- **Sync notice**, one line, only for an expired session or changes the server
  rejected. Pending changes — normal offline use — show nothing.

### Shift form
- Pay preview in the shift's own currency (it used the account currency).
- Bracket lines translated ("125% Overtime") instead of the engine's English
  diagnostic label, each priced at its own multiplier, with night premium on its
  own line so the lines add up.
- The time line lists shift length, break and worked time under the profile's real
  rules.
- Editing a completed shift shows "Currently saved: times · pay" beside the new
  estimate once something pay-related changes. The preview prices the stored row
  until then, matching `saveEditedShift`, which keeps the old compensation snapshot
  for non-pay edits.
- The preview's engine call is remembered rather than re-run on every keystroke.

### Reports
- Overview first: worked hours and estimated gross, then paid time (if different),
  paid leave on its own line and never added to worked time, and "Month in
  progress" for the current month. The phone's four stat cards, which repeated the
  legend, are gone.
- **How your pay was estimated**, per currency: regular, overtime, weekend/rest
  day, holidays & premiums, night premium, total, then any deductions the user set
  up. The previous card labelled weekend and holiday money together as "Holiday"
  and left night premium out, so its parts did not add up. The PDF uses the same
  rows.
- **How paid time was counted** when rounding, minimums or paid breaks applied.
- **Job scope** chips with two or more jobs. Only the reported shifts are narrowed;
  the pay-week context is not, so a shift prices the same in every scope (tested:
  the jobs add up to the all-jobs report). Leave follows the job's workplace. A
  deleted job's shifts stay under "All jobs" rather than being filed under another
  job; a stale selection falls back to "All jobs"; an empty one-job month offers to
  show all jobs.
- **Export card** states month, job scope and currencies. CSV appends a "Worked
  Hours" column — existing columns keep their positions for imports that read by
  index — and names the job on the TOTAL row and in the file name. The PDF names
  the job and prints worked and paid time.

### Onboarding and work profile settings
- Overtime, premiums, breaks and rounding, sick pay and deductions fold behind one
  row that says what it holds and that the values come from the region. Nothing was
  removed.
- Plain labels, e.g. "Overtime starts after (hours a day)", "Round paid time",
  "Minimum paid time per shift", "When several premiums apply".
- Changing a job's currency says past shifts will show in it too, unconverted.

### Sync and Wear
- Status strings distinguish *saved on this device / waiting to upload / couldn't
  upload / synced / sign in again*, and "Sync now" reports what the run achieved.
- The watch distinguishes a punch the phone took ("Clocked in") from one only
  queued on the watch ("Clocked in · saved on watch"). The flag is `@Transient`, so
  the phone/watch wire format is unchanged.

## 3. Defects found and fixed on the way

These were found while doing the UX work and are correctness or data-safety
problems, not polish:

| Defect | Effect | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `clockOut` wrote `breakMinutes = 0, notes = null` by default and no caller passed them | Every clock-out erased a break or note recorded on the running shift, including the Paid Projects clock-in note | Null now means "keep" |
| Replayed watch punch-in matching a completed shift's start | `REPLACE` on `(userId, startTime)` deleted the completed shift and opened a new one, later pushed over the server copy | `clockIn` returns the recorded shift, as manual entry did |
| Onboarding replay | Saved the region preset's rules over the default profile, erasing tuned rules the wizard never shows | Rules kept unless the region changes |
| Onboarding rate field | "62,5" saved as 625 | Shared `DecimalInput` (`.`, `,`, Arabic separator, Arabic-Indic digits) in every pay number field |
| Profile overtime-hours field | Shown with the device separator ("8,60" in Russian) and impossible to edit back | Formatted with `.`; input parsed by `DecimalInput` |
| "Fixed per shift" deduction label | The engine charges it once per month per profile | "Fixed amount per month" |
| Work profile form | System back gesture and "Add job" discarded unsaved edits; a saved form still read as changed | Guarded; values compared as saved |
| New premium profile | Took the default from the existing one, lifting its delete protection | Default only when none exists |
| Sync status after a run | Rows held for retry read as "Synced just now" | Reported as waiting |
| Expired session status | "We'll try again automatically" | "Sign in again" |
| Reports month-over-month | Compared last month's clock time (project time included) with this month's payable wage minutes | Worked vs worked |
| Reports shift-row "+OT" | A third overtime rule (`net − raw daily standard`) | Classifier overtime |

## 4. Decisions and things deliberately not done

- **Single-select job scope, not multi-select.** With one or two jobs — the common
  case — "several" is "all". Multi-select would add state and a harder empty state
  for little value.
- **Recap held in memory.** It answers "what did I just do?" and is not restored
  after process death. Headless clock-outs (notification, widget, watch) do not
  produce one.
- **Net pay is not shown as net.** Deductions are user-configured estimates, so they
  appear as "Deductions you set up" and "After those deductions", never as
  take-home pay.
- **No Paparazzi goldens recorded.** `docs/ux-ui-review-2026-08.md` (backlog item 4)
  records that goldens are host-specific; re-recording here would only move the
  failure. Goldens covering the dashboard, reports, shift form and compensation
  screens **are expected to change** and should be re-recorded on the CI image.
- **Tablet Reports** keep their layout; labels and the pay card were updated, and
  export moved to the shared card.

## 6. Remaining issues

- **Translations** for Hebrew, Arabic and Russian were written for this change and
  have not been reviewed by native speakers. Arabic in particular should be reviewed
  before release.
- **Visual review.** Layouts use existing tokens and components and are covered by
  Robolectric render tests (including RTL at 2× font), but no device screenshots
  were reviewed in dark theme.
- **Replay seeding.** An account whose settings have no `regionCode` seeds the replay
  wizard with Israel; confirming would switch a non-Israeli profile's region and
  rules. Rare (very old accounts), pre-existing, and worth fixing by seeding from the
  default profile.
- **Tombstone collision.** The replayed-punch guard covers live shifts; a replay
  matching a *deleted* shift's start time still reaches `REPLACE`.
- **Sync coverage.** `SyncHealth` and the status counts still cover shifts, claims,
  settings, profiles, premiums, tasks and the user profile — not projects, billing,
  payments, workplaces or leave tables.
- **Wear settings** "Sent to watch at…" still appears when the publish failed
  silently; tile punch failures are haptic only.
- **Onboarding copy**: the review step still lists "Name" and "Optional features",
  which the four-step wizard does not ask for.

## 7. Wave 3 readiness (payslip reconciliation)

Prerequisites, not implemented:

1. **A stable per-shift and per-month pay record.** Reconciliation compares a payslip
   with what the app said at the time. Compensation snapshots exist per shift, but
   live profile rules win over them (`CompensationResolver`), so a month's estimate
   moves when rules are edited later. A frozen monthly estimate (or a rule that
   closed months price from their snapshots) is needed first.
2. **Components that map to payslip lines.** `PayComponents` (regular, overtime,
   weekend/rest, holidays & premiums, night, deductions) is the right shape; payslip
   line types and their mapping per region still need defining.
3. **Paid time vs worked time per period** — now available from `MonthlyReport`
   (`workedMinutes`, rounding, minimum top-up, paid breaks).
4. **Leave valuation** is estimate-only and per workplace; a payslip pays leave per
   employer, so the job ↔ workplace link must be reliable for every profile.
5. **Currency per payslip** — one payslip, one currency, one job: the job scope added
   here is the natural unit.
